Diffusion Labs builds sovereign AI that brings intelligence to every part of a nation's economy.

We are an AI research and deployment lab. We believe the value of AI will be set less by what frontier models can do and more by how widely and how well that capability spreads through the economy: into governments, into industries, and into people's daily lives.

Our focus is sovereign AI: intelligence built for a nation's language, laws, and institutions, running on infrastructure it controls. It connects the government, the companies in an industry, and the people they serve in one shared system.

About Diffusion Labs

Every major technology has changed the world through diffusion rather than at the moment it was invented. Electricity took decades to reshape the factory floor. The internet took years to reach most households. AI will follow the same pattern unless someone does the hard work of building it into real institutions and workflows.

That work looks different in every country. A lawyer in Riyadh, a clinic in Cairo, and a ministry in Abu Dhabi each have their own language, their own regulations, and their own trust requirements. General-purpose models built elsewhere don't meet those needs on their own.

We do the research and engineering to close that gap. We adapt and train models for local context and build products on top of them. Then we deploy those products into the sectors that matter most to a nation's economy.

How sovereign AI connects

For each industry we serve, we build one intelligence layer that works for all three parties the industry depends on.

Government. Regulators and ministries use it to encode policy, oversee compliance, and deliver public services at national scale, with data that stays inside the country.

Industry. Firms and institutions run their workflows on shared, compliant infrastructure, connected directly to the regulators above them and the customers they serve.

People. Citizens get services that used to be out of reach, such as legal help and healthcare guidance, in their own language and at the moment they need them.

All three parties work from the same system, so a new regulation, a company's filing, and a citizen's question are all handled in the same place.

Products

Introducing HAQQ

HAQQ (حق, "right" or "truth") is our first product. It is sovereign legal intelligence for the region. Law firms and in-house legal teams use it for research, drafting, and compliance, and government bodies use it to interpret and administer the law. Most importantly, it gives ordinary people access to legal understanding in their own language.

Research

Our research agenda follows what it takes to deploy AI at national scale:

  1. Sovereign models. Adapting and training models for regional languages and dialects, national legal corpora, and cultural context.
  2. Domain reasoning. Reliable, citable reasoning in high-stakes fields like law and medicine, where errors are costly.
  3. Agentic workflows. Systems that carry out multi-step work across government, enterprise, and consumer interfaces.
  4. Evaluation & trust. Benchmarks built with practitioners, plus auditability that institutions can depend on.
  5. In-country infrastructure. Efficient inference that keeps data, compute, and control within national borders.

What we believe

Sovereignty by design. Data, models, and control stay with the nation they serve.

Access is the benchmark. We judge our work by who can use it, not only by what it scores.

Research that ships. Every line of inquiry ends in something people use.
